Questions

Before you start spying.

Still unsure? Ask in the chat any time, or email us. A real person steps in whenever you need one.

Meta’s Ad Library is free. Why pay for Spyvoo?

The Ad Library shows ads, but not which ones work. Its videos and links expire, and ads disappear when the advertiser stops them. Spyvoo saves every video and image forever, gives each ad a winner score, explains in plain English why it works, writes your own version, and alerts you when a competitor launches something new. Hours of scrolling become a few minutes.

Where do the ads come from?

From Meta’s public Ad Library: the same public ads anyone can look up, collected every few hours. Spyvoo never shows private data like a competitor’s budget, targeting or results.

How do I know an ad is a winner?

Nobody keeps paying for an ad that loses money. The winner score (0 to 100) looks at how many days an ad has run, how many versions the advertiser made, how many countries it runs in, and whether it’s still live. An ad still running after 30, 60 or 90 days is very likely profitable. We never invent spend or ROAS numbers, because Meta doesn’t publish them.

Which countries can I search?

Every country where Meta runs ads, from Brazil and Mexico to Egypt, the Gulf, India and Southeast Asia. Search one market or compare fifty at once.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, forever. 10 searches a day, 20 saved ads and 5 AI breakdowns a month. No card needed. Upgrade only when you want more.

Which plan should I pick?

Start on Free and find your first winner. Pick Starter if you run a few offers on your own. Pick Pro if you buy ads full time and launch new creatives every week: unlimited searches and saves, plus far more AI breakdowns, downloads and scripts. Pick Agency if a team of up to 3 people shares boards and limits.

Can I download the videos?

Yes, on Starter and up. Use them to study the hook, pacing and offer, then make your own version. Re-uploading someone else’s ad as your own breaks our rules and Meta’s.

How do I pay? I’ve never paid with crypto.

With USDT (on the TRON network) or Bitcoin, through Gatevoo, our own checkout. You see the exact amount and address, send it from your wallet or exchange, and your plan switches on by itself as soon as the payment confirms. Some countries also see local options like bank transfer. Stuck? The chat checks your payment for you.

Will I be charged automatically? How do I cancel?

Nothing charges you automatically, so there’s nothing to cancel. We email you a few days before your plan ends. Renew if you want to keep going; if not, you move to Free and your boards and saved ads stay.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, on your first paid order. If Spyvoo isn’t for you, ask within 7 days and we refund it, as long as you’ve used less than 20% of the plan’s AI breakdowns or downloads. Double payments are always refunded. See the refund policy.
