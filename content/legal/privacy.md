**{{legal_name}}, {{city}}** runs Spyvoo and is responsible for your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** name, email, country, password (stored only as a secure hash) or your VooSquare account ID.
- **Billing:** the plan you buy, amounts, order references and, for crypto payments, the coin, network and transaction ID. We never see card or bank details for crypto payments.
- **How you use Spyvoo:** searches, saved ads, boards, followed advertisers, alerts and AI requests, so we can run your plan limits and features.
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser type and login times, for security.

## Ad data

Spyvoo shows ads that advertisers published in Meta's public Ad Library, including page names and pictures they made public. If you are an advertiser and want something removed, write to {{privacy_email}}.

## How we use your data

To run your account and plan, send the emails you expect (receipts, alerts, password resets, weekly reports you can switch off), keep Spyvoo secure, and improve it. We don't sell your personal data.

## Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run Spyvoo, under contract: hosting (Railway), email delivery, AI processing (Anthropic, for ad analysis; ad text and transcripts only, not your personal details), payments (Gatevoo) and, if you use it, VooSquare (Zedapex's shared login). We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

While your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep for tax and accounting.

## Your choices and rights

You can see and change your details in **Account**, switch off weekly reports, download your boards, or delete your account. You can also ask us for a copy of your data or to correct or delete it: {{privacy_email}}.

## Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you logged in, and no advertising cookies.
